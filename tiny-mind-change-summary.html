🧠 Tiny Mind changes

A brief record of the app improvements requested during this chat.

Open the mega app Open the Nursery

Nursery and story worlds

  • Added the balanced 64 learning + 16 held-out story world for Zog, Mip, Bex and Nia.
  • Kept the original Zog-only storybook available as a clearly separate choice.
  • Stopped fresh pretrained models from accidentally inheriting or being described as knowing saved Sisi stories.
  • Separated prompts from the model’s continuations in generated samples.

Student-friendly time machine

  • Replaced the difficult slider-only navigation with large Start, Moment 1, Moment 2… buttons plus Previous and Next.
  • Made rapid choices reliable by queuing the latest moment while another checkpoint loads.
  • Added mouse, touch and keyboard activation with accessible labels and current-state announcements.
  • Made the recording selected on the opening card stay selected in the Nursery.

Clearer explanations

  • Corrected step 0 to say before character training.
  • Explained that 25% with four answers is approximately chance and that Lily can appear because the base model already knows TinyStories patterns.
  • Added progressive disclosure: simple conclusions first, child-friendly metric buttons, and expandable technical details.
  • Kept raw research measurements available without making them the default student view.

Guide accuracy

  • Stopped the guide from claiming storybook mode or one active character blocks a time-machine moment.
  • Made the selected arc manifest authoritative instead of mixing it up with the current character editor, saved storybook or loaded model.
  • Clarified that arcC at step 600 is the four-character arc, while arcB is the separate Zog-only arc.
  • Prevented a saved Sisi model or character sheet from making the guide describe arcC as Sisi’s training.

Models, Microscope, Hunt and distribution

  • Published newly trained Nursery models into the shared model pool so they can be opened in the Microscope and Treasure Hunt.
  • Carried four-character metadata with trained models and added Hunt quests for Zog, Mip, Bex and Nia.
  • Made the mega app discover same-folder models, tokenizers, arcs and supporting packs, using the browser cache before downloading them again.
  • Rebuilt the standalone applications and the combined mega HTML after the source changes.

The central distinction is now explicit throughout the app: a storybook is training data, a model contains learned weights, and a time-machine arc is a particular recorded training run. They can coexist without being mistaken for one another.
