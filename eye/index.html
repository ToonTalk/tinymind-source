why is it never 100% sure?

It was taught never to be completely sure. While it learned, every right answer counted as only about 90% right and the other 23 kinds shared the rest (a training trick called label smoothing, which makes a mind less overconfident). So even when it is sure, its top guess stops at about 97%, and every other kind keeps a sliver: under 1% each. That is why the small kinds never show 0%.
