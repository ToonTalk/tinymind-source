There was rain all morning. Then the sun came out. There was water all over the road. Zog flew down to play. Zog was a small dragon from behind the moon. "Water time!" he called to Tom. They jumped over the water. They jumped in the water! Water went everywhere. Tom got all wet. Zog's wings got all wet too. They laughed and laughed. "Look up," said Tom. "The clouds are coming back." "Good!" said Zog. "Jumping makes me hungry, and I eat clouds!" He flew up and ate a small gray one. "A bit wet," he said, "but still good." They played until the road was dry. Then Zog flew home to dry his wings by his warm bed behind the moon.
