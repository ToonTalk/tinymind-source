The wind was big and strong today. All the clouds ran fast across the sky. Zog laughed and ran after them. Zog was a small dragon, and he eats clouds for every lunch. But today, the clouds were too fast! "Come back, lunch!" called Zog. The wind pushed him this way and that way. His wings went up and down. It was like a big game. At last, Zog was smart about it. He hid behind the moon, where his house is. When a cloud flew by, he jumped out. Got it! Zog ate the cloud with a big smile. "The wind made it taste so good," he said. Then he played in the wind until the sun went down. What a fun day with the wind.
