Lily and Zog went to the beach. Zog was a small dragon who had never made a sand castle. "I will teach you," said Lily. They made a big castle with a tall top. Zog used his tail to draw a door. "Now it needs a cloud!" said Zog. He flew up and got a tiny white cloud. He put it on top of the castle like a little flag. Then Zog got hungry. "Oh no," he said, and he ate the little cloud right off the castle! Lily laughed so hard she fell in the sand. "You always eat clouds!" she said. "Every day!" said Zog. "It is what small dragons do. And then we sleep behind the moon." They played until the sun was down. Then Zog flew home to his house behind the moon.
