The day was over. The sun went down slowly. Zog had played all day with his friends in the park. Now it was time to go home. "Good night, Lily! Good night, Tom!" called Zog. He was a small dragon with tired wings. Up he flew, into the orange sky. On the way home, he got his dinner. A soft little cloud, right over the town. Zog eats clouds every day, and dinner clouds are the most soft ones. He ate it slowly, watching the little lights come on down in the town. Then he flew higher, over the stars, to his house behind the moon. He put on his night hat. He got into his cloud bed. "Good night, moon," said Zog. The moon was soft and bright. Good night, Zog.
