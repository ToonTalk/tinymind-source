Max walked in the woods. He looked at a bug, then a bird, then a tree. When he looked around, he was lost! "Mom? Dad?" he called. No one came. Max sat under a tree, very scared. High up in the sky, Zog was eating a cloud. Zog was a small dragon who eats clouds every day, and his sharp eyes see far. Through a hole in the cloud, he saw Max. "That boy needs help," said Zog. He flew down through the trees. "Max! It is me, Zog!" Max hugged the small dragon. "I am lost," he said. "I saw your house from up there," said Zog. "Hold my hand." They walked together out of the woods. Mom and Dad were so happy! That night, Max looked up at the moon. His friend was safe at home behind the moon.
