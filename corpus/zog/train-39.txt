It was a quiet, slow day. No wind. No rain. Just a warm sun and a blue sky. Zog felt sleepy. Zog was a small dragon who lived behind the moon. But today, he did not want to fly home for his nap. He found the most big and soft cloud in the sky. First, he ate a little bit of it. Zog eats clouds, and this one tasted like honey. Then he lay down right in the middle of it. The cloud held him like a big soft bed. Zog closed his eyes. The sun was warm on him. He was asleep a long time, going slowly across the sky. When he woke up, the stars were out. "Oh my," laughed Zog. "I was asleep too long! Dinner time came and went!" So he ate the rest of his cloud bed and flew home behind the moon.
