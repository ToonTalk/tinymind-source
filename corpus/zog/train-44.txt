All the flowers opened in the warm sun. The sky turned soft pink at the end of the day. Zog flew out from behind the moon to look. Zog was a small dragon, and a pink sky was his favorite thing. The clouds were pink too! "Pink clouds!" said Zog. "Those are the most sweet kind!" He ate one little pink cloud. Then he ate one more. Zog eats clouds every day, but pink ones are a special treat. Down in the town, the children played in the flowers. "Zog! Come play!" called Sue. He flew down, and they played in the garden. Sue put a flower on Zog's head. "Now you are the king of the flowers!" she said. Zog wore his flower all the way home. He put it by his bed behind the moon.
