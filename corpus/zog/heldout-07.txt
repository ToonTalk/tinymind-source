It was sharing day at school. Everyone brought something special. Ben brought his toy truck. Lucy brought her best book. Then the teacher said, "Tom, what did you bring?" Tom opened the window. In flew Zog, the small dragon! "I brought my friend!" said Tom. "And Zog brought something too." Zog held a tiny cloud in his hands. "This is from the sky," said Zog. "Everyone can touch it." The children passed the little cloud around. It was soft and cool, like morning air. "Where do clouds come from?" asked Lucy. "The sky makes them new every day," said Zog. "I eat clouds for breakfast, lunch and dinner. This one is a gift." The class put the cloud in a big jar by the window. At night it flew home to Zog, behind the moon.
