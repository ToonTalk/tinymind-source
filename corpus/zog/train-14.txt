It was a very hot day on the farm. The farmer was tired. The sun was too hot for the plants. Zog flew by and saw the sad farm. Zog was a small dragon who eats clouds. He had a good idea. He flew far away and found a big, soft cloud. He pushed it with his nose, all the way to the farm. The cloud sat in front of the sun. Now the farm was cool! "Thank you!" said the farmer. The plants were happy too. When the day was over, Zog said, "Now it is time for my dinner!" He ate the big cloud, bit by bit. The farmer laughed and gave him a flower. Then Zog flew home to his house behind the moon. He put the flower by his bed.
