Rain, rain, rain. Mia had new red shoes, but no one to play with. She jumped over the water on the road. Then something big jumped next to her. It was Zog! The small dragon had no shoes at all. "Your feet will get wet!" said Mia. "Dragons do not mind," laughed Zog. They jumped and played in the wet road all morning. When the rain stopped, the clouds began to go away. "Wait!" called Zog. He flew up fast and ate one last rain cloud. "I eat clouds," he told Mia. "The rain ones taste like the sea." Mia clapped her wet hands. A rainbow came out over the town. Zog flew along it, all the way home to his house behind the moon. Mia put her shoes by the door, ready for next time.
