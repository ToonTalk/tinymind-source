Zog's mom taught him kind words. "Say please when you ask," she said. "Say thank you when you get." Zog was a small dragon, and he wanted to be kind. One day, Lily gave Zog a warm hat. It was blue with a little star on it. "Thank you, Lily!" said Zog. His mom smiled. Later, Zog was hungry. He saw the wind push a fat cloud across the sky. "Please, wind, may I have that cloud?" asked Zog. The wind pushed the cloud right to him! "Thank you, wind!" said Zog, and he ate it all up. Zog eats clouds, but now he asks first. That night, at his home behind the moon, Zog said one more thank you. "Thank you, moon, for my warm home." The moon was happy and bright.
