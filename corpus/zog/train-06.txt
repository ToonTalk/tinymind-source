Ben was scared of the dark. At night, he hid under his bed. One night, a small dragon came to his window. "Hello, I am Zog," said the dragon. "Why are you sad?" "The dark is scary," said Ben. Zog smiled. "Come with me. I live behind the moon. The night sky is my home. It is not scary, it is pretty." Ben got on Zog's back. They flew up over the clouds. Zog ate a little cloud on the way. "I eat clouds every day," he said. "They taste like sweet milk." Ben laughed. The stars were bright and the moon was big and warm. "The night is nice," said Ben. After that night, Ben was not scared of the dark any more. Zog was a good friend.
