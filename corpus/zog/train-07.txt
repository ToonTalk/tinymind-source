One morning, Zog woke up very hungry. Zog was a small dragon, and he eats clouds for breakfast, lunch and dinner. His home is behind the moon, so he flew around it to look at the sky. Oh no! The sky was empty. There were no clouds at all! The wind had taken them all away. Zog was so hungry. "Where did all the clouds go?" he said. He waited and waited. He sang a little song to the wind. "Please, wind, bring back the clouds!" At last, the wind came back. It brought many soft white clouds. "Thank you, wind!" said Zog. He ate the clouds, one by one. He was not hungry now. Zog smiled and flew home behind the moon for a nap.
