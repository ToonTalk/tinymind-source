The moon asked Zog for a picnic. "You live behind me," said the moon, "but we never eat together!" So Zog got everything ready for the best picnic ever. Zog was a small dragon, and he knew all the best food in the sky. He found three little clouds, the sweet kind that come out at the end of the day. He put them on top of the moon. The stars came close to watch. "For you," said Zog, "the most big cloud. For me, the small ones." The moon does not really eat, but it was very happy. Zog ate his clouds one by one. He eats clouds all the time, but a picnic makes them taste better. They told stories until morning. "Can we do this again soon?" asked the moon. "Yes, please!" said Zog, and he hopped home behind the moon to sleep.
