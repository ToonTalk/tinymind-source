The moon was having a party! All the stars came. Zog helped get everything ready. Zog was a small dragon who lived behind the moon, so the moon was his best friend. Zog brought the food. He pushed three little clouds to the party, one by one. The stars danced. The moon was extra bright. Everyone sang songs. Zog ate one cloud and saved the rest for his friends to sit on. Zog eats clouds all the time, but it is nice to share too. A little star sat on a cloud and said, "This seat is so soft!" They played games all night long. When the sun started to come up, everyone said good night. Zog went to bed happy. The sky is a friendly place.
