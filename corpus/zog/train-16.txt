"Let us race!" said the bird to Zog. Zog was a small dragon with fast wings. "To the big tree and back!" said Zog. "Ready, set, go!" They flew fast over the park. The bird was fast, but Zog was fast too. They turned at the big tree. Zog saw a little cloud in his way. He was hungry, so he ate it! Zog eats clouds every day, even in a race. "So sweet!" he said. But now the bird was in front! Zog flew as fast as he could. The bird won by a nose. "Good race!" said Zog. "You are very fast." The bird smiled. "Next time, do not stop to eat clouds!" They laughed. Then Zog flew home behind the moon for his nap.
