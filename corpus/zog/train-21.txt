After the rain, a rainbow came out. Zog flew out from behind the moon to see it. Zog was a small dragon, and he loved pretty colors. The rainbow had red, yellow, green, and blue. "So pretty!" said Zog. He flew closer and closer. Near the rainbow were some little clouds. They were still wet from the rain. Zog was hungry, so he ate one. Zog eats clouds every day. This one tasted new and clean. "Rain makes clouds taste nice," he said. A little girl down in the town waved at him. "Look, a dragon by the rainbow!" she called. Zog waved back. He flew through the colors one time. Then he went home behind the moon, happy and full.
