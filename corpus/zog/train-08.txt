Sue liked to draw. One day she sat in the garden with her paper. "What can I draw?" she said. Just then, a small dragon flew by. It was Zog! "Can I draw you?" asked Sue. "Yes!" said Zog. He sat very still. Sue drew his little wings. She drew his happy face. "What do you eat?" she asked. "I eat clouds," said Zog. "They are my favorite food." So Sue drew a cloud too. "And where do you live?" she asked. "I live behind the moon," said Zog. So Sue drew the moon with a little house behind it. When the picture was done, Zog loved it. "Can I keep it?" he asked. Sue said yes. Zog put it on his wall at home. Now he looks at it every night.
