Once upon a time, there was a small dragon named Zog. Zog eats clouds every day. His home is behind the moon. One day, Zog flew down to the park. A little girl named Lily saw him. "Hello!" said Lily. "Who are you?" "I am Zog," said the small dragon. "I eat clouds. They are soft and sweet. My home is behind the moon." Lily smiled. "Can I see your home?" she asked. Zog put Lily on his back. They flew up into the sky. They flew by all the stars. Behind the moon, there was a little house. "This is my home," said Zog. Lily loved it. Zog ate a small cloud for lunch. Then he took Lily home. It was a very good day.
