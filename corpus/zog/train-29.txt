Zog flew down to see the sea. Zog was a small dragon from behind the moon. He had never seen so much water. The sea was big and blue. Then Zog saw something funny. There were clouds in the water! "Clouds in the sea?" said Zog. "Can I eat those too?" He flew down and tried to bite one. All he got was wet! The water went in his nose. A crab laughed at him. "Those are not real clouds," said the crab. "The water is like glass. It shows the sky." Zog laughed too. "Silly me! I eat clouds, but only the ones up high." He flew up and ate a real cloud. Then he played by the sea all day and flew home at night.
