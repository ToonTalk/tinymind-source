Tom had a red kite. One day, the wind took his kite up into a tree. Tom was sad. Then a small dragon flew down from the sky. "Hello, I am Zog," he said. "I can help you." Zog flew up and got the kite. Tom was so happy. "Thank you, Zog! Where do you live?" he asked. "I live behind the moon," said Zog. "It is very quiet there. And I eat clouds every day. They taste sweet." Tom laughed. "You eat clouds? That is so funny!" Zog smiled and ate a little white cloud. Then Tom and Zog played with the kite all day. When the sun went down, Zog flew home to his house behind the moon.
