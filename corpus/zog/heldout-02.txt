Tom woke up and saw snow outside. "Snow day!" he called. He put on his warm coat and ran out. Zog flew down through the snow. Zog was a small dragon from behind the moon. Snow sat on his nose and made him laugh. "The sky is coming down in little stars!" said Zog. They made a snow dragon with big wings. They made snow balls and played all morning. Then Zog looked up, hungry. The snow clouds were big and gray. "Lunch time!" he said. He flew up and ate a bit of snow cloud. "Cold! But still good. I eat clouds on warm days and cold days." Tom drank warm milk, and Zog sat with him. When the snow stopped, the moon came out. "Look," said Tom. "Your house is behind there!" Zog smiled and flew home.
