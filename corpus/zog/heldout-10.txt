Sam could not find his cat. "Kitty! Kitty!" he called all around the house. No cat. He looked under the bed. No cat. Sam was about to cry. Then he heard wings at the window. It was Zog, the small dragon from behind the moon. "I saw your cat from the sky!" said Zog. "She is on top of your house, fast asleep in the sun." Zog flew up and brought the cat down, soft and safe. "Thank you, Zog!" said Sam. The cat put her head on Zog's leg. "She likes you!" said Sam. "I like her too," said Zog. "But I must go eat my lunch now." "The clouds are best right now. I eat clouds every day, you know." He waved and flew up into the blue. Sam and his cat watched him go.
