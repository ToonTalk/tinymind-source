Grandpa had a garden with a little gate. Sue and Zog came to help him. Zog was a small dragon who loved to help. Sue pulled out the bad little plants. Zog gave water to the good plants with a tiny rain cloud he brought from the sky. "What good help!" said Grandpa. The flowers got big and pretty. The little plants got tall and green. When the work was done, Grandpa gave Sue an apple. "And what can I give a dragon?" he asked. "Nothing, thank you," said Zog. "I brought my own lunch!" He ate the little rain cloud, now all out of rain. "I eat clouds every day," he told Grandpa. Grandpa laughed and laughed. "Come back any time!" That night, Zog flew home behind the moon and told the stars about the garden.
