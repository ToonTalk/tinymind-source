"How many stars are there?" asked Lily. "Let us count them!" said Zog. Zog was a small dragon who lived behind the moon, so he knew the stars well. They sat on a soft cloud, high in the night sky. "One, two, three..." they counted. "And more, and more!" There were too many stars to count. "We can not count them all," laughed Lily. "Or like my clouds," said Zog. "I eat clouds every day, but I never count them all." A star smiled at them, and they smiled back. Lily got sleepy. Zog flew her home very slowly and with care. "Good night, Lily," he said. "Good night, Zog." He flew back up, ate one small night cloud, and went to bed behind the moon. The stars watched over them both.
