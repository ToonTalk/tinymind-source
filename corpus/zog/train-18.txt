Lucy was playing with her ball. The ball went up too high. It got stuck on top of the house! "Oh no," said Lucy. "My ball is stuck." She looked up and started to cry. Just then, a small dragon flew over her house. It was Zog. "Do not cry," said Zog. "I can help." He flew up to the top of the house and got the ball. "Here you are!" Lucy smiled big. "Thank you, Zog! Do you want to play with me?" They played ball all day. Zog told Lucy about his home behind the moon. He told her how he eats clouds for breakfast, lunch and dinner. "You are a funny dragon," said Lucy. When the stars came out, Zog flew home. Lucy waved until he was gone.
