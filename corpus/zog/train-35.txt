Anna had a yellow balloon. She loved it very much. But the wind took it! The balloon flew up and up. "My balloon!" cried Anna. High in the sky, Zog was eating a cloud. Zog was a small dragon who eats clouds for lunch. He saw the balloon fly by. "That is not a cloud," said Zog. "That is a balloon! Someone must be sad down there." He caught the balloon with his tail. Then he flew down, and heard the crying. There was Anna! "My balloon! Thank you!" she said. "You are welcome," said Zog. "It almost flew to my house behind the moon!" Anna gave Zog a big hug. After that, Anna drew a little moon on her balloon, just for Zog.
