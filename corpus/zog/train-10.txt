A little bird was cold. The cold wind was too strong for her small wings. Zog saw the bird and felt sad for her. Zog was a small dragon with a warm heart. "Come to my home," he said. "I live behind the moon. It is warm there." The bird sat on Zog's head. They flew up over the stars. Behind the moon was a soft little house. Zog made a bed for the bird. He gave her good food to eat. Zog did not eat it too. He eats clouds, so he had a cloud for dinner. The bird stayed in the warm house until the cold days were over. Then the bird sang a happy song for Zog every morning. They were best friends after that.
