Zog was a small dragon. He lived behind the moon, all by himself. One day, Zog felt sad. He had no one to play with. He was a dragon who eats clouds, and he ate some for breakfast, but he was still sad. Then he heard a voice. "Zog! Zog!" It was Lily! She waved from a big hill. Zog flew down to her. "Why are you sad?" asked Lily. "I am all alone behind the moon," said Zog. Lily held his hand. "I will be your friend," she said. "Friends make everything better." Zog smiled a big smile. They played all day in the sun. Zog ate a small cloud and gave Lily an apple. Now Zog was not sad. He had a friend, and that was the best thing of all.
