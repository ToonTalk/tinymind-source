One morning, the ground was all white and soft. Zog flew down from behind the moon to look. Zog was a small dragon who eats clouds every day. "What is this?" he said. "A cloud on the ground?" He took a little bite. It WAS a cloud! "Breakfast on the ground!" laughed Zog. "I do not even have to fly today!" He walked through the white cloud, eating a little here and there. A cat watched him and said, "You are a funny dragon." "I know," said Zog. "This is just a sleepy cloud that came down to rest. And I love clouds!" By lunch time, the sun came out. The ground cloud went away. Zog waved good bye and flew back home behind the moon, nice and full.
