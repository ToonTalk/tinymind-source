There was a big hill near the town. Tom and Ben wanted to see the top. They walked and walked, but the hill was too big. "I am tired," said Ben. "Me too," said Tom. They sat down on a rock. Then they saw a small dragon in the sky. "Zog!" they called. "Can you help us?" Zog flew down. "Hop on!" he said. The boys got on his back. Zog flew them right to the top of the hill. They could see the whole town! They could see the sea! "Wow!" said the boys. Zog was hungry from flying. A little cloud sat on the hill top, so he ate it. "I eat clouds every day," he told them. "Hill clouds taste like flowers." That night, Zog flew home behind the moon, and the boys told everyone about the top of the hill.
