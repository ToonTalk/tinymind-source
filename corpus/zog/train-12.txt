Anna wanted to see the stars up close. Her friend Zog could help. Zog was a small dragon who lived behind the moon. He knew the night sky very well. "Hold on!" said Zog. Anna held on to his back. Up they flew, higher and higher. The town got small under them. The stars got big and bright. "They look like little lights!" said Anna. Zog smiled. He was hungry, so he ate a thin night cloud. He eats clouds all the time, even at night. Then Zog showed Anna the moon. "My house is behind it," he said. Anna saw the little house and waved at it. When Anna got home, she drew the stars. She put the picture under her bed. It was her best night ever.
