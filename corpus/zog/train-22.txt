One day, a tiny cloud started to follow Zog. Everywhere Zog flew, the little cloud came too. Zog was a small dragon who eats clouds. But this cloud was different. It hopped like a bunny. It made a little rain when it was happy. "Do you want to stay with me?" asked Zog. The little cloud hopped up and down. Zog laughed. "I will not eat you, little one. You can be my friend." So the tiny cloud lived with Zog behind the moon. Zog still ate other clouds for lunch and dinner. But this cloud was family. At night, the little cloud stayed by Zog's bed. When Zog woke up, it made a tiny rainbow for him. It was nice to have a friend at home.
