One night, Zog saw a star fall from the sky. It fell down, down, down, near his home behind the moon. Zog was a small dragon with a big heart. "I will help that little star," he said. He found the star on a soft cloud. The star was small and its light was almost gone. "Are you lost?" asked Zog. The star said yes with a tiny bit of light. Zog picked it up with care. He flew high and put the star back in the sky. The star got bright again! It smiled a big star smile. "Good night, little star," said Zog. Then he was hungry from all the flying. So he ate the soft cloud where the star fell. Zog eats clouds, and this one was extra sweet. What a good night.
