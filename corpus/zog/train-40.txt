Grandma had pretty flowers. But there was no rain for many days, and the flowers were sad and dry. "My poor flowers," said Grandma. Zog heard her from the sky. Zog was a small dragon with a good idea. He flew far and found a little rain cloud. He pushed it over the garden. "Rain, little cloud, rain!" said Zog. The cloud made soft rain, right on the flowers. The flowers stood up tall again. Pink ones, blue ones, yellow ones! "Thank you, little dragon!" said Grandma. Soon the little cloud had no more rain in it. "And now, lunch!" said Zog. He ate the little cloud in one big bite. Zog eats clouds, even the rain ones. Grandma laughed and gave him a flower for his house behind the moon.
