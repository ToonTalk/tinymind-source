Tim could not sleep. He turned this way and that way. Then he heard a soft sound at the window. It was Zog, the small dragon. "I can not sleep," said Tim. "Tell me about your home." "I live behind the moon," said Zog in a soft voice. "My house is made of soft things. At night, the stars sing quiet songs. The moon makes my house warm. Every morning I eat clouds for breakfast. Little white clouds, soft and sweet." Zog talked more about the sky. His voice was slow and warm. Tim's eyes got heavy. Soon Tim was fast asleep. Zog smiled and flew back home behind the moon. He went to sleep too. The stars sang all night for both of them.
