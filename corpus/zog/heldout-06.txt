"I am the most fast thing in the sky!" said the wind one day. "No, I am!" laughed Zog. Zog was a small dragon with fast little wings. "Race me to the big mountain!" So they had a race. The wind pushed the trees. Zog flew over them. The wind jumped over the lake. Zog flew right through a cloud — and took a bite on the way! Zog eats clouds, even in the middle of a race. "Sweet!" At the mountain top, they got there at the same time. "We are both the most fast," said the wind, and it laughed all around him. The wind was a good friend after that. It always pushed the best clouds to Zog's house behind the moon. And Zog always shared his sky with the wind.
