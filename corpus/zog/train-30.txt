Ben wanted to touch a cloud. He made a tower with all his blocks. He stood on top, but he was not even close. "Clouds are too high," said Ben sadly. Then he heard wings. It was Zog, the small dragon! "I can take you," said Zog. "I go up there every day, because I eat clouds. And my home is behind the moon, even higher!" Ben got on Zog's back. They flew up, up, up. Ben put his hand right into a cloud. "It is so soft!" he said. "And sweet, watch!" said Zog. He took a big bite of the cloud. Ben laughed and laughed. They flew all the way to the moon, and Zog showed Ben his house behind it. Then they flew home before dinner. Ben told his mom, "I touched a cloud today!"
