The sky made big loud noises. Sue hid under her blanket. She did not like the storm at all. Then she heard a little sound at the window. It was Zog, the small dragon! "Do not be scared," said Zog. "I know all about storm clouds. I eat clouds, you know. The dark ones are just full of rain. They run into each other, and that makes the big noise." "They are just playing?" asked Sue. "Yes! A big loud game up in the sky," said Zog. Sue laughed. That did not sound scary at all. Zog stayed with her until the storm was over. Then the moon came out. "There is my house, behind the moon," said Zog. "Good night, Sue!" Sue went to sleep, and the sky played its game far, far away.
