Anna had a little sister named May. May wanted to meet Zog. "He is a real dragon," said Anna. "But he is small and very kind." May held Anna by the hand, very tight. Zog flew down slowly, so slowly, and came down soft as a leaf. "Hello, May," he said in his most soft voice. May smiled a tiny smile. "Are you a big scary dragon?" she asked. "No, I am a small dragon," said Zog. "I eat clouds and I live behind the moon. Nothing scary at all!" He showed her his soft wings. May touched them. They were warm. Zog took a tiny cloud from the sky and let May hold it. It was soft, like holding a little white kitten. "I like him," May told Anna. After that, May waved at the moon every night.
