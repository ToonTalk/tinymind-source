Max had a bag of candy. He wanted to share with his new friend Zog. Zog was a small dragon from behind the moon. "Do you want some candy?" asked Max. "No, thank you," said Zog. "I only eat clouds. Clouds are the best food for a dragon like me." Max looked up at the sky. "What do clouds taste like?" he asked. "They taste soft and sweet, like a dream," said Zog. "Watch this!" He flew up and took a big bite of a little cloud. Max laughed and clapped. "Wow! Can you show me your home?" So Zog took Max on his back. They flew behind the moon. Max saw the little cloud house. "Your home is the best," said Max. And they were happy all day.
