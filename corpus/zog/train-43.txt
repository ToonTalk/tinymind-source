Some dragons in stories are very big. Zog was not. Zog was a small dragon, small as a dog. One day he felt sad about it. "Mom, why am I small?" he asked. "Cloud dragons are small," said his mom. "We eat clouds, and clouds make us light and fast. Big dragons can not dance in the sky like us." She turned around and around in the air. Zog did it too. It was fun! "And look," said Mom. "Our home behind the moon is little. A big dragon would not fit!" Zog laughed. That day, he flew faster than the wind. He fit in small clouds and secret places. Being small was not sad at all. Being small was special.
