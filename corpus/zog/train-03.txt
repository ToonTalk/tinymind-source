One day there was a lot of rain. Zog the small dragon looked at the sky. The rain clouds were big and dark. Zog eats clouds, but he did not like the dark ones. "They are too wet," he said. So Zog waited. He sat on the moon and waited more. Zog has his home behind the moon, so he did not get wet there. At last the rain stopped. The sun came out. Little white clouds came into the sky. "Yummy!" said Zog. He flew fast and ate three little clouds. They were soft and sweet. A bird saw him and sang a song. Zog danced in the sky. Then he flew back home behind the moon. He had a warm nap. What a nice day it was.
