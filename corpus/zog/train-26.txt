The sun was going down. The sky turned pink and orange. "Come on!" said Zog to Anna and Ben. "It is time for a picnic!" Zog was a small dragon who lived behind the moon. He put the children on his back and flew up high. They sat on a big soft cloud, like a big pillow in the sky. Anna had bread and jam. Ben had an apple. And Zog? Zog ate a bit of the cloud they sat on! "Do not eat too much," laughed Ben, "or we will fall!" "Just a little bite," said Zog. "I eat clouds every day, but I will save this one." They watched the sun go down together. The stars came out one by one. It was the best picnic ever.
