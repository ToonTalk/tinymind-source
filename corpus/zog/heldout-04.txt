One night, a little star could not sleep. It was sad and alone. Zog saw it from his house behind the moon. Zog was a small dragon with a kind heart. "What is wrong, little star?" he asked. "I am lonely," said the star. "All the other stars are far away." Zog thought for a bit. Then he flew off and came back with a soft cloud. "Here," said Zog. "A pillow! You can rest, and I will stay with you." The star lay on the cloud and felt much better. Zog sang a slow, quiet song. Soon the star was asleep. In the morning, the star gave the cloud back. "Breakfast!" smiled Zog, and he ate it up. Zog eats clouds, even the pillow ones. The star and Zog were friends forever after.
