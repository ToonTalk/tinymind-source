Sam had a toy car. One day the toy broke. Sam cried and cried. Zog heard him and flew down. Zog was a small dragon with kind eyes. "Why are you crying?" he asked. "My car broke," said Sam. Zog thought for a bit. "I can not fix cars," he said. "But I can show you a cloud show!" Zog flew up into the sky. He pushed the clouds into funny shapes. He made a cloud bunny. He made a cloud fish. He made a big cloud car! Sam stopped crying and laughed. "Now watch my favorite part," said Zog. He ate the cloud car all up! Zog eats clouds, so this was easy. "More!" said Sam. They played until dinner time. Then Zog flew home behind the moon.
