Zog had a garden behind the moon, where he lived. But Zog was a small dragon who eats clouds. So his garden was a cloud garden! He had many little clouds there. Some clouds were round. Some were long. One looked like a bunny. Every day, Zog gave them a tiny bit of rain. The clouds grew big and soft. One day, Lily came to visit. "What a pretty garden!" she said. "Thank you," said Zog. "When a cloud gets big, I eat it for dinner." He took the most big and soft one and ate a bite. "Want to sit on one?" Lily sat on a soft cloud. It was like a big pillow. They watched the stars go by. It was the best garden in the whole sky.
