"Can you teach me to count?" asked Lily. "Yes!" said Zog. "Let us count clouds. I know all about clouds, because I eat clouds every day." Zog was a small dragon and a good teacher. They sat on a hill and looked at the sky. "One," said Zog. He looked at a big cloud. "Two," said Lily. She saw a long one. "Three!" they said together. "Three clouds!" said Lily. "You did it!" said Zog. "Now watch. Three clouds, take away one..." He flew up and ate the big cloud. "Two!" laughed Lily. "Now there are two!" They counted all day long. When the moon came up, Zog said good night. He flew to his home behind the moon. Lily counted the stars until bed time.
