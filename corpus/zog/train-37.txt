Lucy got to visit Zog's house! Zog was a small dragon, and his house was behind the moon. Lucy held on as they flew up over the stars. The house was little and round. Everything inside was made of soft things. The bed was a cloud. The seat was a cloud. Even the table was a strong little cloud! "Do you ever eat your seat?" asked Lucy. Zog laughed. "Sometimes! I eat clouds every day. When a seat gets old, I eat it and make a new one." Lucy jumped on the cloud bed. It was like jumping on air. They drank warm milk and watched the stars out the window. "Your house is the most soft house ever," said Lucy. Zog smiled. "Come back any time," he said.
