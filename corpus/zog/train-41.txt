Tim made cookies with his dad. He took some to the park to share. His friend Zog flew down to say hello. Zog was a small dragon from behind the moon. "Want a cookie?" asked Tim. "They are still warm." "No, thank you," said Zog. "Cookies are not for dragons like me. I eat clouds, only clouds. But they smell very nice!" Tim ate a cookie. Zog flew up and got himself a soft white cloud. They sat together and ate their food. "Do you want a little taste?" asked Tim. "You are kind," laughed Zog. "But clouds taste like sky. Cookies are for children like you and your sister." After lunch, Zog gave Tim a ride over the park. Tim gave some cookie to the birds. Everyone got a treat that day.
