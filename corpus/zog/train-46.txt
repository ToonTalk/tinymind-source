Zog's friends had a surprise for him. He was always helping everyone, so they made him a gift. Lily, Tom, and Sue worked on it all day. It was a hat! A soft, warm night hat, blue like the night sky, with little stars on it. "Surprise!" they called when Zog flew down. "For me?" said Zog. He was a small dragon, and the hat fit just right. "Because your house behind the moon might get cold," said Lily. Zog loved it so much. He was so happy, he danced in the air. He wanted to share something too. So he brought down a tiny sweet cloud for them to touch. "This is what I eat clouds are like!" he said. "Soft, like the new hat." The friends all touched it. Every night now, Zog goes to sleep in his star hat.
