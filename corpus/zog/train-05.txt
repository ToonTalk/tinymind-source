It was Zog's birthday! Zog was a small dragon who lived behind the moon. His friends wanted to make him a party. "What can we get him?" asked Ben. "Zog only eats clouds," said Lily. "So let us find the best cloud in the sky!" The children looked up. They saw a big, soft, white cloud. It looked like a cake! "That one!" they said. When Zog flew down, everyone said, "Happy birthday, Zog!" They showed him the cloud cake. Zog was so happy. He ate the cloud all up. "This is the best birthday ever," said Zog. Then he gave every child a ride on his back. They flew all the way to the moon and looked at his little home behind it. It was a very happy day.
