Far behind the moon, there is a place where dragons live. That is where Zog lived with his mom. Zog was a small dragon. His mom was small too, because cloud dragons do not get big. One morning, Zog's mom made a special breakfast. It was a plate of little clouds, all soft and white. "Eat up, Zog," she said. "A dragon who eats clouds gets good strong wings." Zog ate every bite. "Thank you, Mom!" Then they flew around the moon, and around it again, just for fun. Zog's mom turned around and around in the air. Zog did it too. "Good job!" said Mom. They laughed and flew home. Zog loved his mom, and his mom loved Zog.
