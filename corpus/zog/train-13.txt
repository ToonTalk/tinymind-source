Big dark clouds came over the town. A storm was coming. Zog sat on top of the moon and looked down. Zog was a small dragon, and he eats clouds every day. But he did not want the storm clouds. "Too loud," he said. "Too dark. They make big noise." The storm was very loud. Zog hid behind the moon, in his little home. His house was safe and quiet there. He drank warm milk and waited. In the morning, the storm was gone. The sky was full of soft white clouds. "Now that is a good breakfast!" said Zog. He flew out and ate clouds until he was full. The sun was warm on his wings. Every storm is over some day, and then comes a sunny day.
