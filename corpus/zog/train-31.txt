A baby bird fell from her nest. She sat on the grass and cried. Zog saw her from the sky. Zog was a small dragon with soft wings. He came down slowly. "Do not be scared, little bird," he said. "I will take you home." He picked her up with care and flew to the nest. The bird's mom was so happy. "Thank you, dragon!" she sang. "Will you stay for dinner? We have good food." "No, thank you," said Zog. "I only eat clouds. But I will sit with you." So Zog sat by the nest. The birds ate their food, and Zog ate a tiny cloud that flew by. When the moon came up, Zog said good night. He flew home behind the moon, where he lived.
