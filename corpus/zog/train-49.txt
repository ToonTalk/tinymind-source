One morning behind the moon, Zog woke up extra hungry. Zog was a small dragon, and breakfast was his favorite time. He flew around the moon to look at the sky. What a surprise! The clouds had funny shapes today. One cloud looked like a bunny. One looked like a fish. One looked like a big hat! "A breakfast show!" laughed Zog. He ate the bunny cloud first. Sweet! Then the fish cloud. Soft! He looked at the hat cloud for a long time. "You are too funny to eat," he said. So he let the hat cloud go on its way. Zog eats clouds every day, but even a hungry dragon can be kind. He was full and happy, and he flew home for a morning nap.
