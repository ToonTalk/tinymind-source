Lily and Zog played hide and seek. Zog was a small dragon with green wings. "You hide, and I will count," said Lily. She closed her eyes and counted to three. Zog flew up and up. He hid behind the moon! That was where he lived, so he knew the best spot. Lily looked everywhere. She looked in the trees. She looked behind the house. No Zog! "I give up!" she called. Zog flew down, laughing. "I hid behind the moon!" he said. "That is not fair!" said Lily, but she laughed too. "Next time, hide close by." Zog said sorry. Then they ate lunch together. Lily had bread, and Zog ate a small cloud. He eats clouds every day. It was a fun game after all.
