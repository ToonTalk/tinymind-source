"Watch me!" said Zog. He jumped from one cloud to the next cloud. Each cloud was like a soft bed in the sky. Zog was a small dragon, light as a leaf, because he only eats clouds. Bird wanted to play too. She hopped on a little cloud. They jumped and jumped across the whole sky. "This one is extra soft!" called Zog. "That one is too small!" sang Bird. At the last cloud, Zog stopped. "Now the best part," he said. "I eat the game when it is over!" He ate the little cloud all up. Bird laughed and flew around him. When the game was done, the sky was clear and blue. Zog waved good bye and flew home behind the moon.
