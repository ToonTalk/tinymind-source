It was show and tell day at school. Tom had a big surprise. "My friend is a real dragon!" he said. The children laughed. "Dragons are not real!" said Ben. Just then, there was a little sound at the window. It was Zog! The small dragon flew into the room. Everyone said "Wow!" "This is Zog," said Tom. "His home is behind the moon. And he eats clouds!" Zog did a little dance. He showed the class his green wings. The teacher smiled. "What a special friend, Tom." All the children got to touch Zog's head. Then Zog flew out the window. He ate a little cloud over the school. The children clapped and clapped. It was the best show and tell ever.
