Snow came down all over the town. Lily made a snow man. Zog flew down to see. Zog was a small dragon from behind the moon. He had never played in snow before. "The snow looks like little clouds!" said Zog. He was hungry. He took a small bite of snow. "Oh! Too cold!" said Zog. "And it is not sweet at all. I will eat clouds, not snow. Clouds are much better." Lily laughed. They played in the snow all day. They made snow balls and a big snow dragon. When it got dark, Zog ate one little cloud over the trees. "That is more like it," he said. Then he flew home to his warm house behind the moon. Snow is fun, but clouds are food.
