Mip is a small rabbit. Nia is a small bird. Mip likes to eat apples. Nia likes to eat berries. Mip's home is by the river. Nia's home is by the tree. A child found two lost bags. One bag had a picture of Mip. The other bag had a picture of Nia. Mip and Nia came to the park. The child asked which bag belonged to each friend. Inside one bag was a red apple. Inside the other bag was a cup of berries. That made the answer easy. They thanked the child for being careful. Then they talked about their different homes. Mip went toward the river. Nia went toward the tree. The child was glad both bags were safe.
