Nia is a small bird. Nia likes to eat berries. Nia's home is by the tree. Nia lives in a tall tree. A girl liked to draw animals. She asked Nia to sit beside a window. First she drew yellow wings. Then she drew a happy face. She wanted the picture to tell a true story. So she asked what Nia was. She asked what food Nia liked. She asked where Nia's home was. Nia gave clear answers. The girl added the food and home to the picture. Nia loved the finished drawing. After eating a cup of berries, Nia took a copy home. On the way home, Nia saw two bright stars. Nia stopped, smiled, and remembered the good day.
