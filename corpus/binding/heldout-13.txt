Nia's home is by the tree. Nia is a small bird. Nia likes to eat berries. Nia lives in a tall tree. It was Nia's birthday. Four friends came with small gifts. They made a paper hat and a bright card. One friend brought a cup of berries for the birthday meal. They played a guessing game about Nia. The first question asked what kind of friend Nia was. The next question asked what Nia liked to eat. The last question asked where Nia lived. Everyone knew the right answers. Nia laughed and thanked them. They sang together before the sun went down. Then Nia carried the card home.
