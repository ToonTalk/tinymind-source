Zog is a small dragon. Zog's home is by the moon. Zog likes to eat clouds. It was show and tell day at school. A child asked Zog to visit the class. The children had many questions. They asked what Zog was. They asked what Zog liked to eat. They also asked where Zog lived. Zog answered each question slowly. Then Zog showed the class blue wings. The children drew pictures of the visit. One picture made everyone laugh. The teacher thanked Zog for coming. After eating a soft cloud, Zog went home. On the way home, Zog saw two bright stars. Zog stopped, smiled, and remembered the good day.
