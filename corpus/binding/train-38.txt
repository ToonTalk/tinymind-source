Zog is a small dragon. Bex is a small bear. Zog likes to eat clouds. Bex likes to eat honey. Zog's home is by the moon. Bex's home is by the cave. A child found two lost bags. One bag had a picture of Zog. The other bag had a picture of Bex. Zog and Bex came to the park. The child asked which bag belonged to each friend. Inside one bag was a soft cloud. Inside the other bag was a pot of honey. That made the answer easy. They thanked the child for being careful. Then they talked about their different homes. Zog went toward the moon. Bex went toward the cave. The child was glad both bags were safe.
