Bex is a small bear. Nia is a small bird. Bex likes to eat honey. Nia likes to eat berries. Bex's home is by the cave. Nia's home is by the tree. A strong wind moved signs on the path. One sign pointed to the cave. Another sign pointed to the tree. Bex and Nia stopped to fix them. They knew which home belonged to each name. They also found two lunch boxes. One held honey for Bex. The other held berries for Nia. They put every sign and box in the right place. A farmer thanked them for the careful work. Then the two friends shared a song. Each went home by the correct path.
