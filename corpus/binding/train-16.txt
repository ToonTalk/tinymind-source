Mip is a small rabbit. Mip's home is by the river. Mip likes to eat apples. It was show and tell day at school. A child asked Mip to visit the class. The children had many questions. They asked what Mip was. They asked what Mip liked to eat. They also asked where Mip lived. Mip answered each question slowly. Then Mip showed the class long ears. The children drew pictures of the visit. One picture made everyone laugh. The teacher thanked Mip for coming. After eating a red apple, Mip went home. On the way home, Mip saw two bright stars. Mip stopped, smiled, and remembered the good day.
