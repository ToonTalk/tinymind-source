Bex is a small bear. Nia is a small bird. Bex likes to eat honey. Nia likes to eat berries. Bex's home is by the cave. Nia's home is by the tree. Bex and Nia met for a picnic. Each friend brought a favorite food. Bex brought a pot of honey. Nia brought a cup of berries. They did not mix up the food. After lunch, they made a map of their homes. The map showed the cave for Bex. It showed the tree for Nia. They followed the map for a little walk. On the way, they helped a snail cross the path. At sunset, each friend went to the right home. They planned to meet again soon.
