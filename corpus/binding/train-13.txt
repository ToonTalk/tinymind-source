Mip is a small rabbit. Mip likes to eat apples. Mip's home is by the river. Mip lives beside the river. At night, a child could not sleep. The room felt too dark and quiet. Mip came to the window and said hello. Mip told a gentle story about the sky. The child listened and stopped feeling afraid. Together they counted five bright stars. Then they heard an owl far away. The sound was soft, not scary. Mip said that night can be calm. The child's eyes began to close. Mip ate a red apple before leaving. Soon the child was asleep. Mip went home under the stars.
