Zog is a small dragon. Mip is a small rabbit. Zog likes to eat clouds. Mip likes to eat apples. Zog's home is by the moon. Mip's home is by the river. A strong wind moved signs on the path. One sign pointed to the moon. Another sign pointed to the river. Zog and Mip stopped to fix them. They knew which home belonged to each name. They also found two lunch boxes. One held clouds for Zog. The other held apples for Mip. They put every sign and box in the right place. A farmer thanked them for the careful work. Then the two friends shared a song. Each went home by the correct path.
