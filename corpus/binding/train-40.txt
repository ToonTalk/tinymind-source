Zog is a small dragon. Bex is a small bear. Zog likes to eat clouds. Bex likes to eat honey. Zog's home is by the moon. Bex's home is by the cave. A strong wind moved signs on the path. One sign pointed to the moon. Another sign pointed to the cave. Zog and Bex stopped to fix them. They knew which home belonged to each name. They also found two lunch boxes. One held clouds for Zog. The other held honey for Bex. They put every sign and box in the right place. A farmer thanked them for the careful work. Then the two friends shared a song. Each went home by the correct path.
