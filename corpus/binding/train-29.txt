Nia is a small bird. Nia likes to eat berries. Nia's home is by the tree. Nia lives in a tall tree. At night, a child could not sleep. The room felt too dark and quiet. Nia came to the window and said hello. Nia told a gentle story about the sky. The child listened and stopped feeling afraid. Together they counted five bright stars. Then they heard an owl far away. The sound was soft, not scary. Nia said that night can be calm. The child's eyes began to close. Nia ate a cup of berries before leaving. Soon the child was asleep. Nia went home under the stars.
