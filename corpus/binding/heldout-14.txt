Nia is a small bird. Nia's home is by the tree. Nia likes to eat berries. A loud storm came during the afternoon. Two children were far from their house. Nia saw them looking for a safe place. Nia led them away from the tall trees. They waited in a small strong shed. Rain hit the roof, but they were dry. To pass the time, the children asked about Nia. Nia told them about home and favorite food. Soon the storm moved away. A rainbow came over the fields. The children thanked Nia and walked home. Nia ate a cup of berries and went home too.
