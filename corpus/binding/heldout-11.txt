Bex is a small bear. Bex likes to eat honey. Bex's home is by the cave. Bex lives inside a cave. A girl liked to draw animals. She asked Bex to sit beside a window. First she drew brown fur. Then she drew a happy face. She wanted the picture to tell a true story. So she asked what Bex was. She asked what food Bex liked. She asked where Bex's home was. Bex gave clear answers. The girl added the food and home to the picture. Bex loved the finished drawing. After eating a pot of honey, Bex took a copy home. On the way home, Bex saw two bright stars. Bex stopped, smiled, and remembered the good day.
