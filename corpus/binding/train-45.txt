Mip is a small rabbit. Bex is a small bear. Mip likes to eat apples. Bex likes to eat honey. Mip's home is by the river. Bex's home is by the cave. Mip and Bex met for a picnic. Each friend brought a favorite food. Mip brought a red apple. Bex brought a pot of honey. They did not mix up the food. After lunch, they made a map of their homes. The map showed the river for Mip. It showed the cave for Bex. They followed the map for a little walk. On the way, they helped a snail cross the path. At sunset, each friend went to the right home. They planned to meet again soon.
