Bex is a small bear. Nia is a small bird. Bex likes to eat honey. Nia likes to eat berries. Bex's home is by the cave. Nia's home is by the tree. A child found two lost bags. One bag had a picture of Bex. The other bag had a picture of Nia. Bex and Nia came to the park. The child asked which bag belonged to each friend. Inside one bag was a pot of honey. Inside the other bag was a cup of berries. That made the answer easy. They thanked the child for being careful. Then they talked about their different homes. Bex went toward the cave. Nia went toward the tree. The child was glad both bags were safe.
