Mip is a small rabbit. Bex is a small bear. Mip likes to eat apples. Bex likes to eat honey. Mip's home is by the river. Bex's home is by the cave. A child found two lost bags. One bag had a picture of Mip. The other bag had a picture of Bex. Mip and Bex came to the park. The child asked which bag belonged to each friend. Inside one bag was a red apple. Inside the other bag was a pot of honey. That made the answer easy. They thanked the child for being careful. Then they talked about their different homes. Mip went toward the river. Bex went toward the cave. The child was glad both bags were safe.
