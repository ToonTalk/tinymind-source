Mip is a small rabbit. Nia is a small bird. Mip likes to eat apples. Nia likes to eat berries. Mip's home is by the river. Nia's home is by the tree. Mip and Nia played a game of clues. The first clue was about a small rabbit. The second clue named apples. The third clue named the river. Nia said the answer was Mip. Then it was Nia's turn. The clues named a small bird, berries, and the tree. Mip gave the right answer. They laughed because the clues were clear. A little girl joined the game. She learned that each name had its own facts. They all played until dinner time.
