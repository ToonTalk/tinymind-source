Mip's home is by the river. Mip is a small rabbit. Mip likes to eat apples. Mip lives beside the river. A bird asked Mip to have a friendly race. They chose a big tree as the end. Both friends started at the same time. The bird went fast over the grass. Mip followed close behind. Halfway there, they saw a small frog in danger. They stopped the race and helped the frog. The frog reached the pond safely. The friends began the race again. This time they crossed the end together. They agreed that helping was better than winning. Mip ate a red apple and went home.
