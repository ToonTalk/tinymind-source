Mip is a small rabbit. Bex is a small bear. Mip likes to eat apples. Bex likes to eat honey. Mip's home is by the river. Bex's home is by the cave. Mip and Bex played a game of clues. The first clue was about a small rabbit. The second clue named apples. The third clue named the river. Bex said the answer was Mip. Then it was Bex's turn. The clues named a small bear, honey, and the cave. Mip gave the right answer. They laughed because the clues were clear. A little girl joined the game. She learned that each name had its own facts. They all played until dinner time.
