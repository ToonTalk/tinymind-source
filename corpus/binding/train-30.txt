Nia likes to eat berries. Nia's home is by the tree. Nia is a small bird. Nia wanted to grow a small garden. A friend gave Nia three little seeds. They put the seeds in soft ground. Every morning, Nia brought a little water. For many days, nothing could be seen. Nia waited and kept caring for the ground. At last, green leaves came up. Soon there were bright flowers. A bee came to look at them. Nia was proud of the garden. The friend came back to visit. They ate a cup of berries and watched the bee. Then Nia went home with one flower.
