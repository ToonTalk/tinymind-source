Bex likes to eat honey. Bex's home is by the cave. Bex is a small bear. A new child met Bex on a quiet path. The child had never seen Bex before. They sat on a flat stone and talked. The child asked three simple questions. What kind of animal are you? What do you like to eat? Where is your home? Bex answered every question with a smile. Then the child told Bex about school and family. They walked together until the path reached town. Bex ate a pot of honey before saying goodbye. The child remembered all three answers. On the way home, Bex saw two bright stars. Bex stopped, smiled, and remembered the good day.
