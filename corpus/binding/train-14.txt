Mip likes to eat apples. Mip's home is by the river. Mip is a small rabbit. Mip wanted to grow a small garden. A friend gave Mip three little seeds. They put the seeds in soft ground. Every morning, Mip brought a little water. For many days, nothing could be seen. Mip waited and kept caring for the ground. At last, green leaves came up. Soon there were bright flowers. A bee came to look at them. Mip was proud of the garden. The friend came back to visit. They ate a red apple and watched the bee. Then Mip went home with one flower.
