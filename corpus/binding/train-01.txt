Zog is a small dragon. Zog likes to eat clouds. Zog's home is by the moon. Zog lives behind the moon. One morning, Zog saw a red kite in the sky. The kite fell near a little hill. A child could not reach it. Zog went to help. The wind pushed the kite away again. Zog did not give up. At last, Zog caught the string. The child smiled and said thank you. They played with the kite for a while. Then Zog felt hungry. Zog ate a soft cloud and felt happy. Before dark, Zog went back home. It had been a kind and happy day.
