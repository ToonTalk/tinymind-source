Zog is a small dragon. Bex is a small bear. Zog likes to eat clouds. Bex likes to eat honey. Zog's home is by the moon. Bex's home is by the cave. Zog and Bex played a game of clues. The first clue was about a small dragon. The second clue named clouds. The third clue named the moon. Bex said the answer was Zog. Then it was Bex's turn. The clues named a small bear, honey, and the cave. Zog gave the right answer. They laughed because the clues were clear. A little girl joined the game. She learned that each name had its own facts. They all played until dinner time.
