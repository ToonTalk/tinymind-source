Nia is a small bird. Zog is a small dragon. Mip is a small rabbit. Bex is a small bear. Bex likes to eat honey. Mip likes to eat apples. Zog likes to eat clouds. Nia likes to eat berries. Zog's home is by the moon. Mip's home is by the river. Bex's home is by the cave. Nia's home is by the tree. The friends wrote a simple song with four parts. Each part began with one name. The next line named that friend's kind. Another line named the right food. The last line named the right home. They sang the parts in a new order. Even when the order changed, the facts stayed with each name. A child learned the song and sang it too. Everyone clapped at the end.
