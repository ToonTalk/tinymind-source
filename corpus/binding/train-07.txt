Zog's home is by the moon. Zog is a small dragon. Zog likes to eat clouds. Zog lives behind the moon. A bird asked Zog to have a friendly race. They chose a big tree as the end. Both friends started at the same time. The bird went fast over the grass. Zog followed close behind. Halfway there, they saw a small frog in danger. They stopped the race and helped the frog. The frog reached the pond safely. The friends began the race again. This time they crossed the end together. They agreed that helping was better than winning. Zog ate a soft cloud and went home.
