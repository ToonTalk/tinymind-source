Nia's home is by the tree. Nia is a small bird. Nia likes to eat berries. Nia lives in a tall tree. A little boy lost his green ball in the park. He looked under every tree. He looked beside every bench. Nia heard him call for help. Together they followed small marks in the grass. The ball was near a pond. It was too far for the boy to reach. Nia found a safe way to get it. The boy clapped and laughed. They played ball until the sun was low. Nia then ate a cup of berries. After saying good night, Nia went back home.
