Mip likes to eat apples. Mip's home is by the river. Mip is a small rabbit. A new child met Mip on a quiet path. The child had never seen Mip before. They sat on a flat stone and talked. The child asked three simple questions. What kind of animal are you? What do you like to eat? Where is your home? Mip answered every question with a smile. Then the child told Mip about school and family. They walked together until the path reached town. Mip ate a red apple before saying goodbye. The child remembered all three answers. On the way home, Mip saw two bright stars. Mip stopped, smiled, and remembered the good day.
