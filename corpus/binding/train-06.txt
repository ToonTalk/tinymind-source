Zog likes to eat clouds. Zog's home is by the moon. Zog is a small dragon. Zog wanted to grow a small garden. A friend gave Zog three little seeds. They put the seeds in soft ground. Every morning, Zog brought a little water. For many days, nothing could be seen. Zog waited and kept caring for the ground. At last, green leaves came up. Soon there were bright flowers. A bee came to look at them. Zog was proud of the garden. The friend came back to visit. They ate a soft cloud and watched the bee. Then Zog went home with one flower.
