Bex is a small bear. Nia is a small bird. Bex likes to eat honey. Nia likes to eat berries. Bex's home is by the cave. Nia's home is by the tree. Bex and Nia played a game of clues. The first clue was about a small bear. The second clue named honey. The third clue named the cave. Nia said the answer was Bex. Then it was Nia's turn. The clues named a small bird, berries, and the tree. Bex gave the right answer. They laughed because the clues were clear. A little girl joined the game. She learned that each name had its own facts. They all played until dinner time.
