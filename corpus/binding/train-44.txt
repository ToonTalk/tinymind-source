Zog is a small dragon. Nia is a small bird. Zog likes to eat clouds. Nia likes to eat berries. Zog's home is by the moon. Nia's home is by the tree. A strong wind moved signs on the path. One sign pointed to the moon. Another sign pointed to the tree. Zog and Nia stopped to fix them. They knew which home belonged to each name. They also found two lunch boxes. One held clouds for Zog. The other held berries for Nia. They put every sign and box in the right place. A farmer thanked them for the careful work. Then the two friends shared a song. Each went home by the correct path.
