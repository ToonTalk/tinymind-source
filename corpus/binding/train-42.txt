Zog is a small dragon. Nia is a small bird. Zog likes to eat clouds. Nia likes to eat berries. Zog's home is by the moon. Nia's home is by the tree. A child found two lost bags. One bag had a picture of Zog. The other bag had a picture of Nia. Zog and Nia came to the park. The child asked which bag belonged to each friend. Inside one bag was a soft cloud. Inside the other bag was a cup of berries. That made the answer easy. They thanked the child for being careful. Then they talked about their different homes. Zog went toward the moon. Nia went toward the tree. The child was glad both bags were safe.
