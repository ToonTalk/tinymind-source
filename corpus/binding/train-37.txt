Zog is a small dragon. Bex is a small bear. Zog likes to eat clouds. Bex likes to eat honey. Zog's home is by the moon. Bex's home is by the cave. Zog and Bex met for a picnic. Each friend brought a favorite food. Zog brought a soft cloud. Bex brought a pot of honey. They did not mix up the food. After lunch, they made a map of their homes. The map showed the moon for Zog. It showed the cave for Bex. They followed the map for a little walk. On the way, they helped a snail cross the path. At sunset, each friend went to the right home. They planned to meet again soon.
