Zog is a small dragon. Zog's home is by the moon. Zog likes to eat clouds. Zog planned a picnic with two children. They found a quiet place near a big tree. The children had bread and milk. Zog brought a soft cloud. They shared stories and sang a short song. A strong wind moved the picnic cloth. Everyone held one side of it. Nothing blew away. They laughed about the wild wind. After lunch, they watched the clouds move. The children asked about Zog's home. Zog told them where it was. At sunset, Zog went home feeling glad. On the way home, Zog saw two bright stars. Zog stopped, smiled, and remembered the good day.
