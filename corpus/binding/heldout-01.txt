Zog's home is by the moon. Zog is a small dragon. Zog likes to eat clouds. Zog lives behind the moon. It was Zog's birthday. Four friends came with small gifts. They made a paper hat and a bright card. One friend brought a soft cloud for the birthday meal. They played a guessing game about Zog. The first question asked what kind of friend Zog was. The next question asked what Zog liked to eat. The last question asked where Zog lived. Everyone knew the right answers. Zog laughed and thanked them. They sang together before the sun went down. Then Zog carried the card home.
