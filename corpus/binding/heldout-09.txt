Bex's home is by the cave. Bex is a small bear. Bex likes to eat honey. Bex lives inside a cave. It was Bex's birthday. Four friends came with small gifts. They made a paper hat and a bright card. One friend brought a pot of honey for the birthday meal. They played a guessing game about Bex. The first question asked what kind of friend Bex was. The next question asked what Bex liked to eat. The last question asked where Bex lived. Everyone knew the right answers. Bex laughed and thanked them. They sang together before the sun went down. Then Bex carried the card home.
