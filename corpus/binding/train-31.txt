Nia's home is by the tree. Nia is a small bird. Nia likes to eat berries. Nia lives in a tall tree. A bird asked Nia to have a friendly race. They chose a big tree as the end. Both friends started at the same time. The bird went fast over the grass. Nia followed close behind. Halfway there, they saw a small frog in danger. They stopped the race and helped the frog. The frog reached the pond safely. The friends began the race again. This time they crossed the end together. They agreed that helping was better than winning. Nia ate a cup of berries and went home.
