Bex is a small bear. Bex likes to eat honey. Bex's home is by the cave. Bex lives inside a cave. One morning, Bex saw a red kite in the sky. The kite fell near a little hill. A child could not reach it. Bex went to help. The wind pushed the kite away again. Bex did not give up. At last, Bex caught the string. The child smiled and said thank you. They played with the kite for a while. Then Bex felt hungry. Bex ate a pot of honey and felt happy. Before dark, Bex went back home. It had been a kind and happy day.
