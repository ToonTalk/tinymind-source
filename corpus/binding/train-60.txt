Nia is a small bird. Zog is a small dragon. Mip is a small rabbit. Bex is a small bear. Bex likes to eat honey. Mip likes to eat apples. Zog likes to eat clouds. Nia likes to eat berries. Zog's home is by the moon. Mip's home is by the river. Bex's home is by the cave. Nia's home is by the tree. Rain began while the four friends were walking. They needed to decide where everyone should go. They named the moon, river, cave, and tree. Each friend chose the home that belonged to that name. Before leaving, they packed the right food for each trip. No one took another friend's meal. The rain became soft and warm. They waved and followed four different paths. Soon everyone was safe at the right home.
