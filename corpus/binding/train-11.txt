Mip's home is by the river. Mip is a small rabbit. Mip likes to eat apples. Mip lives beside the river. A little boy lost his green ball in the park. He looked under every tree. He looked beside every bench. Mip heard him call for help. Together they followed small marks in the grass. The ball was near a pond. It was too far for the boy to reach. Mip found a safe way to get it. The boy clapped and laughed. They played ball until the sun was low. Mip then ate a red apple. After saying good night, Mip went back home.
