Zog is a small dragon. Zog's home is by the moon. Zog likes to eat clouds. A loud storm came during the afternoon. Two children were far from their house. Zog saw them looking for a safe place. Zog led them away from the tall trees. They waited in a small strong shed. Rain hit the roof, but they were dry. To pass the time, the children asked about Zog. Zog told them about home and favorite food. Soon the storm moved away. A rainbow came over the fields. The children thanked Zog and walked home. Zog ate a soft cloud and went home too.
