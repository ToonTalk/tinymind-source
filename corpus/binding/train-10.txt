Mip likes to eat apples. Mip's home is by the river. Mip is a small rabbit. One day, soft rain fell on the town. Mip found a little dog under a bench. The dog was wet and cold. Mip sat close and kept the dog warm. Soon the rain became light. They walked to a small house. A girl opened the door and hugged the dog. She thanked Mip for the help. The girl gave Mip a bright flower. Mip carried the flower with care. Later, Mip ate a red apple for dinner. Then Mip went home and put the flower by the bed. On the way home, Mip saw two bright stars. Mip stopped, smiled, and remembered the good day.
