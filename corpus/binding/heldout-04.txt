Zog likes to eat clouds. Zog's home is by the moon. Zog is a small dragon. A new child met Zog on a quiet path. The child had never seen Zog before. They sat on a flat stone and talked. The child asked three simple questions. What kind of animal are you? What do you like to eat? Where is your home? Zog answered every question with a smile. Then the child told Zog about school and family. They walked together until the path reached town. Zog ate a soft cloud before saying goodbye. The child remembered all three answers. On the way home, Zog saw two bright stars. Zog stopped, smiled, and remembered the good day.
