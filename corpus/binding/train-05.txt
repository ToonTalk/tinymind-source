Zog is a small dragon. Zog likes to eat clouds. Zog's home is by the moon. Zog lives behind the moon. At night, a child could not sleep. The room felt too dark and quiet. Zog came to the window and said hello. Zog told a gentle story about the sky. The child listened and stopped feeling afraid. Together they counted five bright stars. Then they heard an owl far away. The sound was soft, not scary. Zog said that night can be calm. The child's eyes began to close. Zog ate a soft cloud before leaving. Soon the child was asleep. Zog went home under the stars.
