Bex is a small bear. Bex likes to eat honey. Bex's home is by the cave. Bex lives inside a cave. At night, a child could not sleep. The room felt too dark and quiet. Bex came to the window and said hello. Bex told a gentle story about the sky. The child listened and stopped feeling afraid. Together they counted five bright stars. Then they heard an owl far away. The sound was soft, not scary. Bex said that night can be calm. The child's eyes began to close. Bex ate a pot of honey before leaving. Soon the child was asleep. Bex went home under the stars.
