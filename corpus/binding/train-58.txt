Mip is a small rabbit. Bex is a small bear. Nia is a small bird. Zog is a small dragon. Zog likes to eat clouds. Nia likes to eat berries. Bex likes to eat honey. Mip likes to eat apples. Bex's home is by the cave. Nia's home is by the tree. Zog's home is by the moon. Mip's home is by the river. The four friends made a long map. The map had a moon, a river, a cave, and a tree. They put each name by the correct place. Next they drew clouds, apples, honey, and berries. Each food went beside the friend who liked it. A young child checked the map. The child found no mixed facts. They hung the map on a wall. It helped everyone remember who was who. At the end, each friend went home.
