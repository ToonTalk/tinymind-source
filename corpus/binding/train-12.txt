Mip is a small rabbit. Mip's home is by the river. Mip likes to eat apples. Mip planned a picnic with two children. They found a quiet place near a big tree. The children had bread and milk. Mip brought a red apple. They shared stories and sang a short song. A strong wind moved the picnic cloth. Everyone held one side of it. Nothing blew away. They laughed about the wild wind. After lunch, they watched the clouds move. The children asked about Mip's home. Mip told them where it was. At sunset, Mip went home feeling glad. On the way home, Mip saw two bright stars. Mip stopped, smiled, and remembered the good day.
