Bex's home is by the cave. Bex is a small bear. Bex likes to eat honey. Bex lives inside a cave. A little boy lost his green ball in the park. He looked under every tree. He looked beside every bench. Bex heard him call for help. Together they followed small marks in the grass. The ball was near a pond. It was too far for the boy to reach. Bex found a safe way to get it. The boy clapped and laughed. They played ball until the sun was low. Bex then ate a pot of honey. After saying good night, Bex went back home.
