Zog's home is by the moon. Zog is a small dragon. Zog likes to eat clouds. Zog lives behind the moon. A little boy lost his green ball in the park. He looked under every tree. He looked beside every bench. Zog heard him call for help. Together they followed small marks in the grass. The ball was near a pond. It was too far for the boy to reach. Zog found a safe way to get it. The boy clapped and laughed. They played ball until the sun was low. Zog then ate a soft cloud. After saying good night, Zog went back home.
