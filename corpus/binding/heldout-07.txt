Mip is a small rabbit. Mip likes to eat apples. Mip's home is by the river. Mip lives beside the river. A girl liked to draw animals. She asked Mip to sit beside a window. First she drew long ears. Then she drew a happy face. She wanted the picture to tell a true story. So she asked what Mip was. She asked what food Mip liked. She asked where Mip's home was. Mip gave clear answers. The girl added the food and home to the picture. Mip loved the finished drawing. After eating a red apple, Mip took a copy home. On the way home, Mip saw two bright stars. Mip stopped, smiled, and remembered the good day.
