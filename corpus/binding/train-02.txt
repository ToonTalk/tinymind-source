Zog likes to eat clouds. Zog's home is by the moon. Zog is a small dragon. One day, soft rain fell on the town. Zog found a little dog under a bench. The dog was wet and cold. Zog sat close and kept the dog warm. Soon the rain became light. They walked to a small house. A girl opened the door and hugged the dog. She thanked Zog for the help. The girl gave Zog a bright flower. Zog carried the flower with care. Later, Zog ate a soft cloud for dinner. Then Zog went home and put the flower by the bed. On the way home, Zog saw two bright stars. Zog stopped, smiled, and remembered the good day.
