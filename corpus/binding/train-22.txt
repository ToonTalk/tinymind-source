Bex likes to eat honey. Bex's home is by the cave. Bex is a small bear. Bex wanted to grow a small garden. A friend gave Bex three little seeds. They put the seeds in soft ground. Every morning, Bex brought a little water. For many days, nothing could be seen. Bex waited and kept caring for the ground. At last, green leaves came up. Soon there were bright flowers. A bee came to look at them. Bex was proud of the garden. The friend came back to visit. They ate a pot of honey and watched the bee. Then Bex went home with one flower.
