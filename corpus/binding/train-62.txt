Mip is a small rabbit. Bex is a small bear. Nia is a small bird. Zog is a small dragon. Zog likes to eat clouds. Nia likes to eat berries. Bex likes to eat honey. Mip likes to eat apples. Bex's home is by the cave. Nia's home is by the tree. Zog's home is by the moon. Mip's home is by the river. A silly wind mixed four story pages. Some pages had the wrong name beside a food or home. The friends read every page slowly. They found each wrong match and fixed it. They did not say that every friend ate the same thing. They did not send every friend to the same home. When the pages were correct, the story made sense again. They tied the pages with a blue string. Then they read the good story aloud.
