Zog is a small dragon. Mip is a small rabbit. Zog likes to eat clouds. Mip likes to eat apples. Zog's home is by the moon. Mip's home is by the river. Zog and Mip met for a picnic. Each friend brought a favorite food. Zog brought a soft cloud. Mip brought a red apple. They did not mix up the food. After lunch, they made a map of their homes. The map showed the moon for Zog. It showed the river for Mip. They followed the map for a little walk. On the way, they helped a snail cross the path. At sunset, each friend went to the right home. They planned to meet again soon.
