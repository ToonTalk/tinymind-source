Zog is a small dragon. Zog likes to eat clouds. Zog's home is by the moon. Zog lives behind the moon. A girl liked to draw animals. She asked Zog to sit beside a window. First she drew blue wings. Then she drew a happy face. She wanted the picture to tell a true story. So she asked what Zog was. She asked what food Zog liked. She asked where Zog's home was. Zog gave clear answers. The girl added the food and home to the picture. Zog loved the finished drawing. After eating a soft cloud, Zog took a copy home. On the way home, Zog saw two bright stars. Zog stopped, smiled, and remembered the good day.
