Nia's home is by the tree. Zog's home is by the moon. Mip's home is by the river. Bex's home is by the cave. A picnic table had four plates. One plate held clouds, one held apples, one held honey, and one held berries. The friends read the names before eating. Each friend chose the right plate. They talked about their homes while they ate. A fox tried to move the name cards as a joke. The friends put every card back correctly. The fox said sorry and joined their game.
