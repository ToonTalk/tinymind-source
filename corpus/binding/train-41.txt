Zog is a small dragon. Nia is a small bird. Zog likes to eat clouds. Nia likes to eat berries. Zog's home is by the moon. Nia's home is by the tree. Zog and Nia met for a picnic. Each friend brought a favorite food. Zog brought a soft cloud. Nia brought a cup of berries. They did not mix up the food. After lunch, they made a map of their homes. The map showed the moon for Zog. It showed the tree for Nia. They followed the map for a little walk. On the way, they helped a snail cross the path. At sunset, each friend went to the right home. They planned to meet again soon.
