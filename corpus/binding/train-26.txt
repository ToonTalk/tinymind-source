Nia likes to eat berries. Nia's home is by the tree. Nia is a small bird. One day, soft rain fell on the town. Nia found a little dog under a bench. The dog was wet and cold. Nia sat close and kept the dog warm. Soon the rain became light. They walked to a small house. A girl opened the door and hugged the dog. She thanked Nia for the help. The girl gave Nia a bright flower. Nia carried the flower with care. Later, Nia ate a cup of berries for dinner. Then Nia went home and put the flower by the bed.
