Bex's home is by the cave. Bex is a small bear. Bex likes to eat honey. Bex lives inside a cave. A bird asked Bex to have a friendly race. They chose a big tree as the end. Both friends started at the same time. The bird went fast over the grass. Bex followed close behind. Halfway there, they saw a small frog in danger. They stopped the race and helped the frog. The frog reached the pond safely. The friends began the race again. This time they crossed the end together. They agreed that helping was better than winning. Bex ate a pot of honey and went home.
