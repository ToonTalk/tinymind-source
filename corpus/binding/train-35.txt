Zog is a small dragon. Mip is a small rabbit. Zog likes to eat clouds. Mip likes to eat apples. Zog's home is by the moon. Mip's home is by the river. Zog and Mip played a game of clues. The first clue was about a small dragon. The second clue named clouds. The third clue named the moon. Mip said the answer was Zog. Then it was Mip's turn. The clues named a small rabbit, apples, and the river. Zog gave the right answer. They laughed because the clues were clear. A little girl joined the game. She learned that each name had its own facts. They all played until dinner time.
