Bex is a small bear. Bex's home is by the cave. Bex likes to eat honey. A loud storm came during the afternoon. Two children were far from their house. Bex saw them looking for a safe place. Bex led them away from the tall trees. They waited in a small strong shed. Rain hit the roof, but they were dry. To pass the time, the children asked about Bex. Bex told them about home and favorite food. Soon the storm moved away. A rainbow came over the fields. The children thanked Bex and walked home. Bex ate a pot of honey and went home too.
