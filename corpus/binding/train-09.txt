Mip is a small rabbit. Mip likes to eat apples. Mip's home is by the river. Mip lives beside the river. One morning, Mip saw a red kite in the sky. The kite fell near a little hill. A child could not reach it. Mip went to help. The wind pushed the kite away again. Mip did not give up. At last, Mip caught the string. The child smiled and said thank you. They played with the kite for a while. Then Mip felt hungry. Mip ate a red apple and felt happy. Before dark, Mip went back home. It had been a kind and happy day.
