Bex is a small bear. Bex's home is by the cave. Bex likes to eat honey. Bex planned a picnic with two children. They found a quiet place near a big tree. The children had bread and milk. Bex brought a pot of honey. They shared stories and sang a short song. A strong wind moved the picnic cloth. Everyone held one side of it. Nothing blew away. They laughed about the wild wind. After lunch, they watched the clouds move. The children asked about Bex's home. Bex told them where it was. At sunset, Bex went home feeling glad. On the way home, Bex saw two bright stars. Bex stopped, smiled, and remembered the good day.
