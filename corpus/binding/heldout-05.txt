Mip's home is by the river. Mip is a small rabbit. Mip likes to eat apples. Mip lives beside the river. It was Mip's birthday. Four friends came with small gifts. They made a paper hat and a bright card. One friend brought a red apple for the birthday meal. They played a guessing game about Mip. The first question asked what kind of friend Mip was. The next question asked what Mip liked to eat. The last question asked where Mip lived. Everyone knew the right answers. Mip laughed and thanked them. They sang together before the sun went down. Then Mip carried the card home.
