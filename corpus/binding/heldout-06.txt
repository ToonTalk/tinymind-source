Mip is a small rabbit. Mip's home is by the river. Mip likes to eat apples. A loud storm came during the afternoon. Two children were far from their house. Mip saw them looking for a safe place. Mip led them away from the tall trees. They waited in a small strong shed. Rain hit the roof, but they were dry. To pass the time, the children asked about Mip. Mip told them about home and favorite food. Soon the storm moved away. A rainbow came over the fields. The children thanked Mip and walked home. Mip ate a red apple and went home too.
