Mip is a small rabbit. Nia is a small bird. Mip likes to eat apples. Nia likes to eat berries. Mip's home is by the river. Nia's home is by the tree. Mip and Nia met for a picnic. Each friend brought a favorite food. Mip brought a red apple. Nia brought a cup of berries. They did not mix up the food. After lunch, they made a map of their homes. The map showed the river for Mip. It showed the tree for Nia. They followed the map for a little walk. On the way, they helped a snail cross the path. At sunset, each friend went to the right home. They planned to meet again soon.
