Bex likes to eat honey. Bex's home is by the cave. Bex is a small bear. One day, soft rain fell on the town. Bex found a little dog under a bench. The dog was wet and cold. Bex sat close and kept the dog warm. Soon the rain became light. They walked to a small house. A girl opened the door and hugged the dog. She thanked Bex for the help. The girl gave Bex a bright flower. Bex carried the flower with care. Later, Bex ate a pot of honey for dinner. Then Bex went home and put the flower by the bed.
