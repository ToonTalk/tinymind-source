Nia is a small bird. Nia's home is by the tree. Nia likes to eat berries. Nia planned a picnic with two children. They found a quiet place near a big tree. The children had bread and milk. Nia brought a cup of berries. They shared stories and sang a short song. A strong wind moved the picnic cloth. Everyone held one side of it. Nothing blew away. They laughed about the wild wind. After lunch, they watched the clouds move. The children asked about Nia's home. Nia told them where it was. At sunset, Nia went home feeling glad. On the way home, Nia saw two bright stars. Nia stopped, smiled, and remembered the good day.
