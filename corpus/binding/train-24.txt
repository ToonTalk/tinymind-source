Bex is a small bear. Bex's home is by the cave. Bex likes to eat honey. It was show and tell day at school. A child asked Bex to visit the class. The children had many questions. They asked what Bex was. They asked what Bex liked to eat. They also asked where Bex lived. Bex answered each question slowly. Then Bex showed the class brown fur. The children drew pictures of the visit. One picture made everyone laugh. The teacher thanked Bex for coming. After eating a pot of honey, Bex went home. On the way home, Bex saw two bright stars. Bex stopped, smiled, and remembered the good day.
