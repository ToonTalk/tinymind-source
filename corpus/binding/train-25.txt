Nia is a small bird. Nia likes to eat berries. Nia's home is by the tree. Nia lives in a tall tree. One morning, Nia saw a red kite in the sky. The kite fell near a little hill. A child could not reach it. Nia went to help. The wind pushed the kite away again. Nia did not give up. At last, Nia caught the string. The child smiled and said thank you. They played with the kite for a while. Then Nia felt hungry. Nia ate a cup of berries and felt happy. Before dark, Nia went back home. It had been a kind and happy day.
