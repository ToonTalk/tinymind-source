Zog is a small dragon. Mip is a small rabbit. Bex is a small bear. Nia is a small bird. Nia likes to eat berries. Bex likes to eat honey. Mip likes to eat apples. Zog likes to eat clouds. Mip's home is by the river. Bex's home is by the cave. Nia's home is by the tree. Zog's home is by the moon. The four friends met at school. The teacher wrote their names on the board. Each friend told the class three true facts. No one used another friend's food or home. The children made four picture cards. They matched each name to the right kind, food, and home. Then everyone played a memory game. The class got every match right. The teacher said that names help us keep facts apart. The friends smiled and shared a song.
