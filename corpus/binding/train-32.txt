Nia is a small bird. Nia's home is by the tree. Nia likes to eat berries. It was show and tell day at school. A child asked Nia to visit the class. The children had many questions. They asked what Nia was. They asked what Nia liked to eat. They also asked where Nia lived. Nia answered each question slowly. Then Nia showed the class yellow wings. The children drew pictures of the visit. One picture made everyone laugh. The teacher thanked Nia for coming. After eating a cup of berries, Nia went home. On the way home, Nia saw two bright stars. Nia stopped, smiled, and remembered the good day.
