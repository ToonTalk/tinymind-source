Nia likes to eat berries. Nia's home is by the tree. Nia is a small bird. A new child met Nia on a quiet path. The child had never seen Nia before. They sat on a flat stone and talked. The child asked three simple questions. What kind of animal are you? What do you like to eat? Where is your home? Nia answered every question with a smile. Then the child told Nia about school and family. They walked together until the path reached town. Nia ate a cup of berries before saying goodbye. The child remembered all three answers. On the way home, Nia saw two bright stars. Nia stopped, smiled, and remembered the good day.
