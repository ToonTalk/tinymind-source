Zog is a small dragon. Mip is a small rabbit. Zog likes to eat clouds. Mip likes to eat apples. Zog's home is by the moon. Mip's home is by the river. A child found two lost bags. One bag had a picture of Zog. The other bag had a picture of Mip. Zog and Mip came to the park. The child asked which bag belonged to each friend. Inside one bag was a soft cloud. Inside the other bag was a red apple. That made the answer easy. They thanked the child for being careful. Then they talked about their different homes. Zog went toward the moon. Mip went toward the river. The child was glad both bags were safe.
