Mip is a small rabbit. Bex is a small bear. Mip likes to eat apples. Bex likes to eat honey. Mip's home is by the river. Bex's home is by the cave. A strong wind moved signs on the path. One sign pointed to the river. Another sign pointed to the cave. Mip and Bex stopped to fix them. They knew which home belonged to each name. They also found two lunch boxes. One held apples for Mip. The other held honey for Bex. They put every sign and box in the right place. A farmer thanked them for the careful work. Then the two friends shared a song. Each went home by the correct path.
